## Data
- `public/films.tsv` lists film titles, years, directors and one short, well-known line each, shown as the daily quote. The quotations remain the property of their rights holders and are included only as brief quotations. If you are a rights holder and want a line removed, open an issue.
- Film frames on the home screen are fetched at run time from TMDB, Wikimedia Commons and Wikipedia; they are not distributed with the app. If you supply a TMDB key, this product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
- The structure examples describe well-known films in the app's own words. The screenplays themselves are not included; the app only links to places that host them.
